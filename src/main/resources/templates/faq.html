Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using SmartCare.

SmartCare is an integrated healthcare management and telemedicine platform that connects patients with doctors, hospitals, pharmacies, diagnostic centres and ambulance teams in one place — appointments, video consultations, medicine ordering, health records, reviews and more.

Anyone can create an account from the Create account page. Patients are activated instantly and can log in right away. Provider accounts — Doctor, Hospital, Pharmacy, Diagnostic Centre and Ambulance Service — are reviewed and approved by an administrator first, so that patients only ever see verified providers.

Once logged in as a patient, open Doctors to search by specialty, then book an appointment slot. For remote visits, use Telemedicine to start a secure video/audio consultation with your doctor. You will receive reminders before your appointment.

The AI Symptom Checker helps you understand possible causes of your symptoms and suggests which type of specialist to see. It is for guidance only and is not a medical diagnosis — always confirm with a qualified doctor, and call emergency services for anything urgent.

Yes. Browse the Pharmacy store to order medicine, and use Medicine Reminders to schedule doses and keep a history of what you have taken — helping you stay on track with your prescriptions.

Open Ambulance Booking, share your location, and request the nearest available ambulance using our ride-sharing style model. You can track the request status until the team arrives.

You can pay for services and donate to crowdfunding campaigns using bKash or a bank/card through our secure checkout. In this project build, payments run in a clearly labelled demo/sandbox mode — no real money is transferred.

Access is controlled by role-based permissions, passwords are stored using one-way BCrypt hashing, and your medical records are only visible to you and to doctors you explicitly grant access to. You can revoke a doctor's access at any time from your Health Profile.

Reach out to our support team at support@smartcare.local and an administrator will help you recover access or resolve any issue with your account.
